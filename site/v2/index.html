The programme

A structured 12-week programme.

Cohorts of 20 businesses, delivered through a blended model of training, mentorship, digital enablement and supplier readiness — with milestones tracked throughout.

12 weeks 20 businesses per cohort Virtual or in person
WEEK 1

Needs analysis & baseline

Every business completes a diagnostic and is placed on a pathway matched to its maturity, industry and your objectives.

WEEKS 2–10

Training, enablement & mentorship

WEEKS 11–12

Readiness & final reporting

Supplier readiness, Dealroom preparation, progress assessment and the final evidence pack.

GRADUATION

Certificates, profiles & showcase

Completion certificates, corporate-ready supplier profiles and a graduation showcase with your stakeholders.

Tailored development

No two businesses start in the same place.

Even within the same industry, businesses face different challenges. The needs analysis places each one on a pathway — and one cohort can include all three.

FOUNDATION
“The basics are in place.”

For businesses building their digital and compliance foundation.

  • Compliance documentation
  • Professional email & domain
  • Digital basics & record keeping
OPTIMISATION
“The business runs on systems.”

For businesses ready to operate more efficiently.

  • Quoting & invoicing
  • CRM & bookkeeping
  • WhatsApp Business & online presence
SCALE
“Ready for the next opportunity.”

For businesses preparing for corporate supply chains.

  • Capability statement
  • Supplier profile & KPIs
  • Procurement readiness & Dealroom
Core modules

Modules selected by need, not by default.

The programme is the toolkit. The needs analysis tells us which tools each business actually needs.

Development that stays. Software licences are included for 12 months, then transferred to the business.
01

ICT Tools & Productivity

Digital literacy, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, professional email and domains.

02

Cybersecurity & Communications

Cybersecurity awareness and basic controls, plus VoIP and business communications readiness.

03

Business Systems & CRM

Quoting, invoicing, CRM (HubSpot, Zoho or equivalent) and workflow automation.

04

Financial Management & Compliance

Bookkeeping tools and support with CIPC, SARS and B-BBEE affidavit guidance.

05

Sales & Digital Presence

WhatsApp Business, websites and online profiles that help businesses find and serve customers.

06

Procurement Readiness & Mentorship

Supplier onboarding requirements, business coaching, mentorship and growth planning.

Evidence built in
